CalledMe 是你的 AI 会议副驾驶，让你在每一场线上会议中从容不迫。

【开会时，它在默默帮你记录】
• 实时语音转写：基于 Apple 最新设备端语音识别，会议说的话实时变成文字，全程在本地完成，音频不上传任何服务器

【被叫到时，它第一时间提醒你】
• 姓名提醒：三层智能检测，会议中有人提到你的名字（包括同音误读），立即弹窗提醒
• 上下文速览：弹窗附带被叫瞬间的截图、原话和前后对话，3 秒恢复上下文

【散会后，纪要已经写好了】
• 一键 AI 摘要：要点、决策、行动项自动提炼

• 完整历史档案：每场会议的转录、议题、决策、截图完整保存，支持导出 Markdown / HTML / TXT

【为大内存 Mac 而生：AI 也可以完全本地】
• 本地大模型：Apple Silicon 统一内存加持，可直接连接本机运行的 Ollama / LM Studio / MLX 等大模型，转写 + 分析全链路零云端、零 API 费用、离线可用
• 云端自由选：同样兼容任意 OpenAI 接口（DeepSeek、通义、Kimi、OpenAI 等），本地与云端随心切换

【隐私优先，数据完全属于你】
• 语音识别全程在设备端完成，原始音频不离开你的 Mac
• 搭配本地大模型可实现 100% 离线运行，敏感会议内容永不触网
• 会议数据保存在本地，API 密钥存储于 macOS 系统钥匙串

## Promotional Text (max 170 chars)
Zoned out in a meeting and got called on? CalledMe transcribes live, alerts you the moment someone says your name — with a screenshot and full context — and writes the minutes for you.

## Description (max 4000 chars)

CalledMe is your AI meeting copilot, keeping you sharp in every online meeting.

WHILE YOU'RE IN THE MEETING
• Live transcription: Powered by Apple's on-device speech recognition, speech becomes text in real time — entirely on your Mac. Audio never leaves your device.
• Automatic topic tracking: AI follows the discussion and labels the current topic as it shifts.
• Smart screenshots: Captures your screen every 30 seconds (duplicates skipped automatically). Never lose a slide or chart again.

WHEN SOMEONE CALLS YOUR NAME
• Name alerts: A three-layer detection pipeline catches your name the moment it's spoken — even homophone mispronunciations.
• Instant context: The alert includes a screenshot from that exact moment, the original sentence, and the surrounding conversation. Back up to speed in 3 seconds.
• Question extraction: AI pulls out the questions directed at you, so you know exactly what to answer.

WHEN THE MEETING ENDS, THE MINUTES ARE DONE
• One-tap AI summary: Key points, decisions, and action items, automatically distilled.
• Screenshot intelligence: Multimodal AI interprets your captures — charts, data, even who's speaking on screen.
• Full meeting archive: Transcripts, topics, decisions, and screenshots for every meeting. Export to Markdown, HTML, or plain text.

BUILT FOR BIG-MEMORY MACS: AI CAN RUN ENTIRELY LOCALLY
• Local LLMs: Thanks to Apple Silicon unified memory, connect to models running on your own machine — Ollama, LM Studio, MLX, and more. Transcription plus analysis with zero cloud, zero API fees, fully offline.
• Cloud when you want it: Works with any OpenAI-compatible API (DeepSeek, Qwen, Kimi, OpenAI, etc.). Switch between local and cloud anytime.

PRIVACY FIRST
• Speech recognition runs 100% on-device — raw audio never leaves your Mac.
• Pair with a local LLM for a fully offline pipeline — sensitive meetings never touch the network.
• All meeting data stays local. API keys are stored securely in the macOS Keychain.

PERFECT FOR
Online meetings, remote interviews, client calls, project reviews, training sessions, and lectures.

Download CalledMe and make every meeting count.
